Privacy Policy for YT Scheduler

Last updated: July 2026

YT Scheduler is an application that allows users to connect their Google and YouTube accounts to upload videos and schedule videos for publication on their own YouTube channels.

Information We Access

When you connect your Google account, YT Scheduler may request access to:

  • Your basic Google account information, such as your email address.
  • Permission to upload and manage videos uploaded through YT Scheduler.
  • OAuth access tokens required to perform actions authorized by you.

YT Scheduler uses the following Google OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — used to upload and manage videos submitted through the application.

How We Use Information

The information and permissions are used only to:

  • Connect your YouTube account to YT Scheduler.
  • Upload videos selected by you.
  • Set video titles, descriptions, privacy settings, and publishing schedules.
  • Maintain the connection required to complete authorized uploads.

Data Sharing

YT Scheduler does not sell your personal information. We do not share your Google user data with third parties except when required to provide the application's functionality, comply with applicable law, or protect users and the application from abuse.

Data Storage and Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect account information and OAuth credentials. Users should not share their access tokens or account credentials with other parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us using the email address below.

You may also revoke YT Scheduler's access at any time through your Google Account security settings.

Google API Services

YT Scheduler's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
